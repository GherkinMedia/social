aucklandpeastraw Save this one for mulching day. Spread pea straw evenly over moist soil, about 5 to 10 cm thick. Keep it slightly away from plant stems and tree trunks so it doesn't trap moisture against them. Top it up as it breaks down. A thicker layer is better for weeds but covers less area.
