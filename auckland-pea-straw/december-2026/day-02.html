aucklandpeastraw Ever wondered where your pea straw comes from? We source it from trusted arable growers whose peas are grown for people to eat. Once the peas are harvested, the leftover stems, leaves and pods are baled and brought to Auckland. Then we deliver them ourselves, straight to your place.
