aucklandpeastraw You wouldn't spend a summer's day out in the sun without sunscreen. Your soil feels the same! ☀️

Bare soil heats up fast and dries out in the wind, so your plants work harder for every drop.

A good thick layer of pea straw:
🌾 Shades the soil and keeps it cooler
💧 Cuts watering by up to 60%
🌱 Keeps weeds down
🪱 Feeds the soil as it breaks down

Spread it over moist soil and top up as it breaks down. It's ideal around tomatoes, strawberries, raised beds and shrubs.
