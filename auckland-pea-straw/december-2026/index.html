Focus

Get Auckland gardens ready for summer and the holidays.

Every week has a seasonal post, a gardening tip, a real Google review and a product or community post, with extra posts on the other days. We show how pea straw keeps soil cool and moist, keeps weeds down and feeds the soil, and that we deliver it ourselves, free on orders over $40 to most areas. Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve are simple greetings with no selling.
