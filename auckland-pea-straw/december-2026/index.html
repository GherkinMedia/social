Focus

Summer gardens that hold their moisture, delivered to your door across Auckland.

December is when Auckland gardens dry out and get ready for guests. We show how one bale of pea straw keeps soil moist, keeps weeds down and feeds the soil, and that we deliver it ourselves, free on orders over $40. We visit every part of our delivery area, share real Google reviews, and keep Christmas Day itself to a simple greeting with no selling.

Promoting

  • Compressed pea straw bales (about 11 kg, covering up to 10 m²)
  • The Convenient Garden Combo (2 bales, Morganics and a free Wonder Weeder)
  • Natural fertilisers: Morganics, Sealord Naked and Fodda
  • Naturally Neem, garden tools and the wool weed mat
  • Free delivery on orders over $40 to most areas from Bombay to Mangawhai
