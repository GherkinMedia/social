bodyfix_christchurch 🎯 WHY DO SO MANY NEW YEAR'S FITNESS RESOLUTIONS FALL APART?

Often, it's because we try to change too much, too quickly.

Going from no exercise to six workouts a week?
Cutting out every food you enjoy?
Expecting instant results?

It's a lot to maintain.

This year, try something different.

💪 Start with two or three workouts each week.
📅 Put your sessions in your calendar.
🎯 Set goals you can measure.
🙌 Ask for support when you need it.
❤️ Celebrate the small wins.

At Bodyfix, we believe lasting progress comes from building habits you can actually stick with.

And you don't have to wait until January to start.
