bodyfix_christchurch 🎄 DON'T LET DECEMBER DERAIL YOUR FITNESS GOALS!

Here are three simple ways to stay accountable through the festive season:

1️⃣ Schedule your workouts at the beginning of each week.

2️⃣ Find a training buddy or book yourself into a group fitness class.

3️⃣ Set a realistic weekly goal, even if it's fewer workouts than usual.

December doesn't have to be a write-off.

A little planning now can help you head into January feeling ready to keep going, rather than feeling like you're starting from scratch.
